I am an incoming PhD student at [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu/) advised by [Qirun Zhang](https://helloqirun.github.io/). My research interests lie at the intersection of cryptography, programming languages, and formal verification, with a current focus on program analysis for finite-field programs.
<br><br>
Before joining Georgia Tech, I worked as a Software Engineer at [Amuse Labs](https://amuselabs.com/). I have also worked with research groups at the [Max Planck Institute for Software Systems](https://mpi-dsg.org/), [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/), and [IIT Gandhinagar](https://iitgn.ac.in/faculty/ee/fac-joycee) on problems in data systems, cryptography, and hardware-aware optimization. In 2025, I graduated summa cum laude from [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/) with a BSc (Honours) and a Postgraduate Diploma in Computer Science, along with minors in Mathematics and Entrepreneurial Leadership. My education at Ashoka was supported by the Deutsche Bank Scholarship.
